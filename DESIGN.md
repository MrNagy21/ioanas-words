# Existing interface system

This documents the incumbent interface for the guided-practice extension. It does not establish a replacement visual identity.

Therapists use the adult workspace in ordinary daylight on phones and computers. Keep the existing light page, white surfaces, ink text, teal navigation, yellow/coral game accents and system sans typography. Surfaces retain 8px corners. Actions now use rounded 14px corners, a darker bottom edge and a short press response inspired by Animata's Duolingo button. Primary actions are coral; secondary actions use a light teal surface with a visible edge. Adult phone actions and the medal action are at least 52px tall. The wheel retains its full palette and pixel illustrations.

Adult pages use a constrained readable workspace with clear headings, separated list rows, and explicit action labels. Phone pages use one column and scroll inside their own viewport container. Desktop places profile navigation beside the selected child's detail. Empty states explain the next action. Saving failures remain visible until resolved. Child identifiers and ratings stay client-side.

Wheel setup keeps Reguli, Evită, Cuvinte and Final in one flat, continuous workspace. Use headings, spacing, thin separators and plain selectable rows; avoid cards within cards across all four steps. Show the chosen size with its unit, such as "8 cuvinte pe roată", and explain the available word pool and wheel maximum separately. Final uses a plain description list and native details for optional saving, retaining update, save-as-new and save-and-start actions. On phones, give Continue/Start more footer width than Back. The saved-configuration heading includes the target; deletion opens with a direct question. Neither needs an eyebrow label. Retain the existing approved pixel pictures, content rules and local-data behavior.

The profile picker uses approved animal pictures from the existing pack. Selected state uses a visible border and text, not color alone. Avoid celebratory motion in adult forms; reserve celebrations for recorded achievements between words. Respect reduced motion and restore focus after closing dialogs.

The selected child opens as a focused mobile detail view with an explicit return to the profile list. Sound settings expand on demand, while the prepare/resume action stays before history. Activity uses a small summary and illustrated word rows. Desktop retains the profile list alongside the child's page. Use the existing coral for start/resume actions and teal for secondary navigation.

Onboarding fills its own viewport with five focused pages and one or two tasks per page: welcome, nickname/avatar or an existing profile, optional sound goal, an exact small picture wheel, and handover to real play. Extend the warm ivory medal background with approved pixel animals and word pictures, ink headings, five-segment teal progress, quiet exit/back controls and a full-width coral next action. Desktop keeps the form capped at 560px inside the wider frame. The wheel-customization hint belongs in the footer above the next action; the footer must not shrink or cover the hint.

Onboarding may scroll on short screens or with enlarged text. Let labels and long names wrap, reduce decorative artwork before controls, and keep every field and action reachable. Each page change resets scroll and focuses its heading. The brief entrance animation runs only when reduced motion is not requested. Help restarts onboarding; exit returns to profiles without losing saved records. An existing round resumes with its history and words intact rather than being replaced by the prepared wheel.

The award screen is child-facing. Use a warm ivory page, a large illustrated medal, a direct "Bravo, [name]!" greeting, the earned milestone and a clear award message. Medal designs are illustrated choices with text and a visible check on the selection. On portrait phones the choices and full-width save action sit below the celebration, near the bottom of the screen. Short landscape uses two columns. Compact portrait reduces the artwork before shrinking controls. Long names or enlarged text may scroll; content must remain reachable. Demo and saved-profile celebrations share this composition. Adult forms stack their main actions on narrow phones; game footers use the actual number of actions.

Missing-page recovery uses the adult page background, ink heading, a small authored wheel illustration and direct navigation. Phone actions fill one column and retain the shared press treatment. Short screens may scroll; recovery never clears local records or requires a profile.
