# Batch 75 Worksheet: High-Value Target Top-Up Candidates

Status: candidate worksheet only; no production content added.

Last updated: 2026-06-23.

## Scope

This worksheet responds to the request to plan more Romanian words for `R`, `S`, `Ș`, `V`, `F`, `L`, `T`, `D`, `Ț`, `H`, `J`, and all enabled sequence groups: `CE`, `CI`, `GE`, `GI`, `CHE`, `CHI`, `GHE`, and `GHI`.

The goal is to prepare reviewable candidate words before any production JSON or image work. These rows are not approved content yet. They need human vocabulary review, source checks where marked, image review, content validation, and the normal production-promotion workflow before entering the child-facing app.

## Source Basis

Sources already recorded in `docs/app-development-program/speech-therapy-content-guidance.md` still apply. This worksheet follows the same therapy-informed direction:

- Romanian logopedie materials prioritize sounds such as `S`, `Z`, `Ț`, `Ș`, `J`, `CE`, `CI`, `GE`, `GI`, `L`, and `R`, and work across initial, medial, and final positions plus differentiation targets.
- Romanian parent/child logopedie material also records contrast work for `S/Z`, `Ș/J`, `Ț` versus `S/CI/Ș`, `CE/CI`, `GE/GI`, `R/L`, `F/V`, and `C/G/H`.
- ASHA's general speech-sound guidance supports checking productions across word positions, phonetic contexts, syllable structures, clusters, and culturally relevant picture prompts.
- Romanian alphabet/source notes keep `K`, `Q`, `W`, and `Y` rare, while `CE`, `CI`, `GE`, `GI`, `CHE`, `CHI`, `GHE`, and `GHI` remain separate practice targets.

## Review Rules For This Batch

- Prefer common, concrete, child-friendly Romanian words.
- Prefer clear single-object or simple-scene images.
- Use `priority` for good production candidates, `reserve` for usable but weaker candidates, and `hold` for words that need human decision before image work.
- Do not force `Ț`, `H`, `J`, `GE/GI`, `CHE/CHI`, or `GHE/GHI` to 25 starts-with rows if the result becomes obscure, abstract, unsafe, or visually unclear.
- For sequence groups, treat `25` mixed usable words as the practical first milestone unless the human explicitly decides that weak starts-with rows are acceptable.
- Plurals are allowed when they are the natural child-known form, but avoid near-duplicate singular/plural padding.
- Cross-listed candidates such as `ceafă`, `săgeată`, `rechin`, and `privighetoare` should be added at most once if accepted; they are repeated only to show which target gaps they can help.
