# Vorbini

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Romanian speech therapists and psychologists guiding children around ages 4 to 6. Parents use the same adult-prepared journey. These facts were confirmed in the product interview in this thread.

## Product purpose

An adult chooses a child and prepares a wheel. Starting the wheel automatically tracks its target for that child. The child spins a picture wheel, practises the word, and collects participation rewards. The adult can see what was practised and record their own pronunciation observations.

## Operating context

Phone portrait comes first, with desktop/tablet support. Adults prepare reusable wheels and select the right local child. Child play has keep/remove actions and a confirmed end. Adult management may scroll.

## Capabilities and constraints

The agreed specification is docs/app-development-program/features/guided-practice-app/spec.md. Extend the existing Next.js app and approved Romanian pack. Keep child records local, preserve anonymous play, and provide password-protected manual backups. No accounts, hosted database, recording, billing, AI scoring, or clinical claims. Phonetic presence and position attribution requires reviewed mappings; chosen wheel-target participation is counted separately. Adult pronunciation observations use the agreed 0–5 labels, start unrated and never change rewards automatically. Romanian mapping review, physical-device acceptance and the therapist pilot remain pending.

Keep the Romanian UI, Ioana Nagy creator credit and approved optimized picture assets. The repository and storage identifiers may retain Word Wheel for compatibility.

## Evidence on hand

596 approved Romanian words with ready images. Existing wheel/setup implementation and regression checks. No completed therapist pilot or clinical efficacy evidence.

## Product principles

- Reward participation without time pressure.
- Keep adult pronunciation judgments separate from automatic counts.
- Make the current child unmistakable and saved data recoverable.
- Preserve every valid saved configuration during migration.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Touch-friendly controls, labelled inputs, keyboard/focus support, reduced-motion equivalents, and optional sound off by default.
