# Word Wheel

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Romanian speech therapists guiding children around ages 4 to 6. Parents use the same adult-prepared journey. These facts were confirmed in the product interview in this thread.

## Product purpose

An adult chooses words and practice goals. The child spins a picture wheel, practises the word, and collects participation rewards. The adult can see what was practised and record their own pronunciation observations.

## Operating context

Phone portrait comes first, with desktop/tablet support. Adults prepare reusable wheels and select the right local child. Child play has keep/remove actions and a confirmed end. Adult management may scroll.

## Capabilities and constraints

The agreed specification is docs/app-development-program/features/guided-practice-app/spec.md. Extend the existing Next.js app and approved Romanian pack. Keep child records local, preserve anonymous play, and provide manual backups. No accounts, hosted database, recording, billing, AI scoring, or clinical claims. Sound attribution requires reviewed mappings. Slider wording and backup protection remain concrete review items.

## Brand commitments

Word Wheel / Roata cuvintelor. Romanian UI and existing creator credit. Extend the current visual system and optimized picture assets.

## Evidence on hand

596 approved Romanian words with ready images. Existing wheel/setup implementation and regression checks. No completed therapist pilot or clinical efficacy evidence.

## Product principles

- Reward participation without time pressure.
- Keep adult pronunciation judgments separate from automatic counts.
- Make the current child unmistakable and saved data recoverable.
- Preserve every valid saved configuration during migration.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Touch-friendly controls, labelled inputs, keyboard/focus support, reduced-motion equivalents, and optional sound off by default.
